01

How the Harvest works

No taxes. No contract tricks. Just pump.fun's own creator rewards, redirected to holders.

STEP 1

People trade $KIN

Every buy and sell on pump.fun — on the bonding curve and after migration — generates creator fees.

STEP 2

Fees hit the Harvest

The creator fees are claimed and sent straight to the public Harvest wallet. Anyone can watch it grow on-chain.

STEP 3

The pot ferments

Until Halloween, the terminal drops daily lore. Paper hands sell; their trades feed the pot even more.

OCT 31

The Reaping

A snapshot is taken at a random block. The entire Harvest is airdropped in SOL, pro-rata, to everyone holding.

02

The rules of the Harvest

Written down now so nobody can move the goalposts later.

harvest.rules

mortal@pump.kin:~$ cat harvest.rules

  1. 100% of creator fees go to the Harvest wallet. The dev keeps nothing.
  2. The Harvest wallet address is published at launch and tracked live in the terminal.
  3. The snapshot happens at a random block on October 31st (UTC) so nobody can buy in at the last second and dump.
  4. Excluded from the snapshot: dev wallet, Harvest wallet, bonding curve / LP, and exchange wallets.
  5. The full pot is distributed in SOL, pro-rata to every remaining holder.
  6. Every payout transaction is published publicly within 24 hours of the Reaping.

FAQ

Where does the money in the Harvest actually come from?

pump.fun rewards coin creators with a share of trading fees on every trade. Normally the dev pockets that. Here, all of it is sent to the Harvest wallet instead. More volume = bigger pot.

Can I verify the pot?

Yes. The Harvest wallet address goes public at launch. You can check it on Solscan any time, and the terminal shows the live balance.

How much will I get?

Your share of the pot equals your share of eligible supply at the snapshot. Hold 1% of eligible supply, receive 1% of the Harvest.

Why a random snapshot?

So people can't ape in one minute before the snapshot and dump the next. If you want the Harvest, hold through October.

What's the terminal?

An AI oracle that lives at /terminal. It drops a new piece of lore every day until Halloween and answers questions — cryptically.
